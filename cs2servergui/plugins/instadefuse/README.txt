cs2-instadefuse — instant bomb defuse for Retakes
==================================================

Repo:    https://github.com/B3none/cs2-instadefuse
Release: https://github.com/B3none/cs2-instadefuse/releases/latest
Current: v2.0.0

Pairs with cs2-retakes. CT defuses complete instantly once the conditions
are met (planted, no Ts alive, etc.).

Wiring
------
Bundled into the Retakes mode plugin list automatically.
