cs2-instaplant — instant bomb plant for Retakes
================================================

Repo:    https://github.com/B3none/cs2-instaplant
Release: https://github.com/B3none/cs2-instaplant/releases/latest
Current: v1.0.0

Pairs with cs2-retakes (the "retakes" plugin folder). Bombs plant
instantly when a T spawns at a site — the classic retakes flow.

Wiring
------
Bundled into the Retakes mode plugin list automatically.
See _MODE_PLUGIN_NAMES["Retakes"] in core.py.
